About Nirala Engineers · Established 1961

We Take This Opportunity To Introduce Ourselves.

WE TAKE THIS OPPORTUNITY TO INTRODUCE OURSELVES AS NIRALA ENGINEERS — one of the pioneers in the field of manufacturing of all types of precision gears, complex import substitute - CNC turned components, custom built gearboxes as well as reconditioning of gearboxes, since 1961.

For more than 5 decades, our company has undertaken and successfully delivered projects to number of machine manufacturing industries nationally as well as globally, and also to government and semi-government concerns. Over these period of years, the company has earned a repute as a consistent quality gear and gearbox supplier.

Our team of qualified employees, experienced engineers and skilled technicians facilitates you with desired national / international standards and specifications. Our clients are assured the highest levels of product quality and workmanship along with on-time completion.
