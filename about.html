Company History · Established 1961

Our History

The journey of Nirala Engineers from 1961 to the present day.

ESTABLISHED 1961

Pioneers in Precision-Gear Manufacturing

With its establishment in 1961, Nirala Engineers began its journey as one of the pioneers in the field of Precision-Gear manufacturing. It caters to a range of products including singular as well as quantum jobs in Gear manufacturing, cnc turned components and reconditioning of all kinds of custom built gear boxes.

VADODARA · FIVE DECADES

Delivering Across India & Globally

Located in the SanskarNagri Vadodara, Gujarat, India, Nirala Engineers has undertaken and successfully delivered projects to various machine-tools manufacturers, government concerns, semi government concerns as well as private undertakings globally. Over five decades, the company has earned a repute as a consistent quality gear supplier throughout India and has a vision to expand the service globally.

1963 · EXPANSION

Swiss Hobbing Machine to Modern Fleet

Nirala Engineers started out as a small-scale industrial unit with just one hobbing machine imported from Switzerland, in 1963. It has expanded over years to house more than ten gear hobbing machines.
