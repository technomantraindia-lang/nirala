About Nirala Engineers

Engineering That Has Earned Its Place.

We are Nirala Engineers — one of the pioneers in manufacturing precision gears, complex import-substitute CNC turned components, custom-built gearboxes, and reconditioned gearboxes since 1961.

For over six decades, we have delivered dependable projects to machine manufacturers, government, and semi-government concerns nationally and globally. Our promise is simple: the highest quality, on time, with a partnership that lasts.
