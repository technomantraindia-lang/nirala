Flexible Gear Couplings.

Crowned-tooth flexible gear couplings engineered to accommodate angular, parallel, and axial shaft misalignments under high torque industrial operations.

Technical Overview & Crowned Tooth Geometry

Precision hobbed internal and external hubs featuring triple-crowned tooth design to distribute load evenly across contact faces, minimizing backlash and stress concentrations under shaft misalignment up to 1.5 degrees per coupling half.

CROWNED TEETH ANGULAR & PARALLEL OFFSET FORGED ALLOY HUBS

Specification & Customization Parameters

Client customizable technical fields. Specify coupling size, bore dimensions, or torque capacity for a prompt quote.

  • COUPLING TYPES: Full Flex (FF) / Flex Rigid (FR) / Spacer Type
  • BORE CAPACITY: 15 mm to 350 mm Bore
  • TORQUE RATING: Up to 450,000 Nm
  • MATERIAL: Forged Steel 45C8 / 42CrMo4 Alloy
  • HEAT TREATMENT: Induction Hardened Teeth 45-52 HRC
  • STANDARDS: AGMA / IS Standards
