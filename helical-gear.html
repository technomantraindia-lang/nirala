Helical Gear Family.

Single and double helical gears with angled teeth engineered for quiet, smooth, high-torque power transmission in heavy industrial gearboxes.

Technical Overview & Manufacturing Route

Our helical gears feature precision angled teeth cut on multi-axis CNC hobbing machines to deliver high contact ratios, reduced tooth stress, and ultra-quiet operating characteristics under heavy loads.

ANGLED TEETH HERRINGBONE / DOUBLE HELICAL QUIET RUNNING

Specification & Customization Parameters

Client customizable technical fields. Submit your drawing or required parameters for an exact quotation.

  • HELIX ANGLE: Left Hand / Right Hand / Custom
  • MODULE / PITCH: 0.8 to 20 Module
  • PRESSURE ANGLE: 20° Normal / Transverse
  • MATERIAL: Case Hardening Steel / 20MnCr5
  • HARDNESS: 58 - 62 HRC Profile Ground
  • STANDARDS: DIN Class 5 / AGMA 12
