Material & Inspection

Spectrometric chemical analysis, ultrasonic internal flaw detection, and micro-structure verification of high-alloy forged steel blanks (EN24, EN353, 20MnCr5) prior to turning.

STAGE 02 / 05

Turning & Shaping

High-precision CNC turning, face profiling, and precision bore sizing held to micro-tolerances within ±0.005mm to ensure perfect concentricity and pitch line runout control.

CNC Face Turning ±0.005mm Tolerance Bore Sizing

STAGE 03 / 05

Gear Cutting & Hobbing

High-speed precision hobbing, Fellows shaping, and spline cutting for involute gear teeth profiles conforming to DIN and AGMA precision manufacturing standards.

High-Speed Hobbing DIN Class 6 Involute Splines

STAGE 04 / 05

Heat Treatment & Hardening

Controlled atmosphere gas carburizing, induction tooth hardening (58–62 HRC), and cryogenic tempering for maximum core toughness and wear resistance under high torque loads.
