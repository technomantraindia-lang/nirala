Production Process

Interactive Production Wheel.

Every Nirala gear passes through five disciplined manufacturing stages to achieve zero backlash, quiet meshing, and long operating life.

PROCESS HUB
STAGE 01 / 05

Material & Inspection

Spectrometric chemical test, ultrasonic flaw detection, and micro-structure verification of high-alloy forged steel blanks prior to turning.

STAGE 02 / 05

Turning & Shaping

High-precision CNC turning, face profiling, and boring to exact tolerances within ±0.005mm.

STAGE 03 / 05

Gear Cutting & Hobbing

High-speed precision hobbing, shaping, and spline cutting for involute gear teeth profiles.

STAGE 04 / 05

Heat Treatment & Hardening

Controlled atmosphere gas carburizing, induction hardening, and tempering for core toughness and surface wear resistance.

STAGE 05 / 05

Teeth Grinding & Dispatch

CNC gear teeth profile grinding to DIN/AGMA quality classes, followed by gear roll inspection and lead error check.

Sequential Breakdown

Full Manufacturing Sequence Details.

1. Raw Material Verification

Incoming forged steel bars and blanks undergo chemical composition spectroscopy, hardness testing, and ultrasonic internal void detection.

2. CNC Turning & Boring

Blank turning on multi-axis CNC machines to achieve precise bore alignment, hub concentricity, and face flatness within ±0.005mm.

3. Precision Gear Hobbing & Shaping

High-speed hobbing machines cut precise spur, helical, spline, and worm teeth geometry according to DIN/AGMA standards.

4. Heat Treatment & Case Hardening

Controlled atmosphere gas carburizing and induction hardening give gear teeth deep wear resistance while keeping the core tough.

5. CNC Teeth Grinding & QA Dispatch

Teeth profile grinding eliminates distortion from heat treatment. Lead error, tooth pitch, and gear roll checks are verified before final rust-preventive packaging and dispatch.
