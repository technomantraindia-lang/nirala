Rack & Pinion.

Straight and helical tooth linear gear racks and matching pinions engineered for accurate positioning in CNC machines, linear stroke units, and industrial automation.

Technical Overview & Precision Linear Motion

Precision CNC milled and gear-ground racks available up to 3 meters in continuous single-length sections. Continuous jointing options allow unlimited stroke lengths for large machine tool beds and gantry cranes.
