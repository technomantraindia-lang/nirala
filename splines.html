Splined Shafts & Hubs.

Involute splines, straight sided splines, and serrated splined shafts manufactured for heavy duty automotive, agricultural, and industrial transmission systems.

Technical Overview & Spline Geometry

Precision gear hobbed external splines and broached/shaped internal splines adhering to DIN 5480, DIN 5482, and ANSI standards. Case hardening and spline grinding guarantee minimal backlash and maximum torque transfer.

DIN 5480 / DIN 5482 INVOLUTE & STRAIGHT BROACHED / HOBBED

Specification & Customization Parameters

Client customizable technical fields. Send pitch diameter, module, tooth count, or drawing details for a quotation.

  • SPLINE TYPE: Involute / Straight-sided / Serrated
  • MODULE / PITCH: 0.8 to 10.0 Module / DP Pitch
  • MAX SHAFT LENGTH: Up to 2000 mm
  • MATERIAL: 20MnCr5 / EN24 / SAE 8620 Alloy
  • HARDENING: Case Carburized 58-62 HRC
  • STANDARDS: DIN 5480 / SAE Standards
