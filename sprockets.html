Drive Sprockets.

Simplex, duplex, and triplex roller chain sprockets engineered for heavy packaging equipment, industrial conveyors, and automated process machinery.

Technical Overview & Manufacturing Route

Precision hobbed teeth profile adhering strictly to British (BS) and American (ANSI) standards. Case-hardened or flame-hardened tooth tips ensure minimal chain wear and extended service lifetime under heavy continuous loads.

ANSI / BS STANDARDS TOOTH TIP HARDENED DUPLEX & TRIPLEX

Specification & Customization Parameters

Client customizable technical fields. Submit your pitch size, tooth count, or custom hub requirements for a prompt estimate.

  • CHAIN PITCH RANGE: 3/8" to 2" Pitch / Custom
  • STRAND COUNT: Simplex, Duplex, Triplex
  • TOOTH COUNT: 9 Teeth to 150+ Teeth
  • MATERIAL OPTIONS: EN8 / C45 / Stainless Steel
  • HUB DESIGN: Type A / Type B / Taper Lock
  • HARDNESS: Induction Hardened 45-55 HRC
