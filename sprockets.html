Technical Overview & Manufacturing Route

We specialize in manufacturing sprockets with dimensions from ¼’’ to 1-1/2’’ PITCH & 6mm PITCH, with outer diameter DIA max up to 1000mm.

Our sprockets are manufactured in various different materials including Carbon Steel (C45 / EN8), High-Tensile Alloy Steels (EN24 / 20MnCr5), Stainless Steel (SS304 / SS316), and Cast Iron FG260, available with or without hardened teeth (induction or flame hardening 45–55 HRC). High precision hobbed tooth profiles ensure low friction, reduced chain stretch, and exceptional durability under harsh continuous industrial operation.
