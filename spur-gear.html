Spur Gear Family.

High-efficiency straight teeth spur gears designed for reliable, positive power transmission across machine tools, textile equipment, and heavy industrial drives.

Technical Overview & Manufacturing Route

Our spur gears are hobbed or shaped on high-precision CNC gear cutting machinery. Available in external and internal tooth configurations, hardened alloy steel, forged steel, or bronze to match your exact load and operating speed requirements.

CNC HOBBED AGMA CLASS 10+ CASE HARDENED

Specification & Customization Parameters

Client customizable technical fields. Submit your drawing or required parameters for an exact quotation.

  • PRESSURE ANGLE: 20° / 14.5° / Specified
  • MAX DIAMETER: Up to 1500 mm
  • MATERIAL: EN8 / EN24 / 20MnCr5 / Bronze
  • HEAT TREATMENT: Induction / Carburized 58-62 HRC
