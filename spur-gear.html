Products / Spur Gear

Spur Gear Family.

High-efficiency straight teeth spur gears designed for reliable, positive power transmission across machine tools, textile equipment, and industrial drives.

Technical Overview & Manufacturing Route

Our spur gears are hobbed or shaped on high-precision CNC gear cutting machinery. Available in external and internal tooth configurations, hardened alloy steel, forged steel, or bronze to match your load and operating speed requirements.
