Gear Tooth Profile Grinding.

Ultra-precision CNC gear grinding service for hardened spur and helical gears to achieve quiet power transmission and extended fatigue life.

Technical Overview & Surface Integrity

Utilizing state-of-the-art CNC generating tooth grinding machines. Corrects heat treatment distortions, eliminates gear noise, and delivers pitch accuracy down to Ra 0.4 µm finish for critical turbine, gear box, and machine tool drives.

DIN CLASS 5 ACCURACY RA 0.4 µM FINISH CROWN & TIP RELIEF

Specification & Customization Parameters

Client customizable technical fields. Send your gear blank or job specifications for contract grinding quotes.

  • MODULE CAPACITY: 1.0 to 22.0 Module
  • MAX GEAR DIA: Up to 1250 mm
  • MAX FACE WIDTH: Up to 500 mm
  • HELIX ANGLE: Up to ±45° Helical
  • PROFILE MODIFICATION: Tip Relief / Crowning / Lead Modification
  • QUALITY STANDARD: DIN 5 / AGMA 12 Class
