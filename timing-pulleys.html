Timing Pulleys.

Precision HTD, STD, and metric timing belt pulleys engineered for zero-backlash positioning and silent power transmission in automated packaging systems.

Technical Overview & Profile Accuracy

Manufactured with specialized gear shaping and hobbing tooling to achieve exact tooth engagement. Eliminates belt slippage, lowers noise, and offers high torque transmission in high-speed machinery.

HTD & STD PROFILES ZERO SLIPPAGE ALUMINUM / STEEL

Specification & Customization Parameters

Client customizable technical fields. Specify pitch size, flange requirements, and bore details for custom quotes.

  • PITCH PROFILES: 3M, 5M, 8M, 14M, T5, T10, AT5
  • BELT WIDTH: 9mm to 115mm Standard
  • FLANGE TYPE: Single / Double Press-fit Flanges
  • MATERIAL: Aluminum 6061 / Mild Steel / EN8
  • FINISH: Anodized / Black Oxide / Plain
  • BORE DETAIL: Pilot / Keyway / Taper Lock
